# Startup Helper

A platform concept designed to help people turn startup ideas into real projects by connecting individuals with complementary skills, knowledge, and interests.

## About the Project

Finding the right people to build a startup with can be difficult. Someone may have a promising idea but lack the technical skills to develop it, while another person may have the skills but no idea or team to work with.

**Startup Helper aims to bridge that gap** by helping people with startup ideas connect with people who have the technical knowledge and skills needed to bring those ideas to life.

The project is being developed as part of the Software Engineering course at the National University of Science and Technology POLITEHNICA Bucharest.

## Project Goals

- Help people find potential co-founders and collaborators.
- Connect startup ideas with people who have relevant technical skills.
- Make it easier to discover projects and find teammates.
- Encourage collaboration between people with complementary skills.

## Project Progress

The project is organized around the course milestones. This section will be updated as the project evolves.

### M1 — Project Description

**Objective:** Define the initial project idea and its value proposition.

This milestone establishes the foundation of the project:

- Introduce the project and its purpose.
- Identify the problem the project aims to address.
- Describe the proposed solution and its value proposition.
- Identify the target customer segments.
- Review existing alternatives and define the project's potential advantages.
- Outline key metrics, costs, and possible revenue streams.
- Present the team and its roles.

[Milestone 1 requirements](https://ocw.cs.pub.ro/courses/se/project/milestones/m1)

### M2 — Idea Validation

**Objective:** Investigate whether the problem is real and whether the proposed solution addresses users' needs.

This milestone focuses on customer discovery:

- Identify and describe the problem and the initial solution hypothesis.
- Define the target customer segments.
- Plan and conduct customer discovery activities.
- Document interviews, questionnaires, and other research methods.
- Present the questions asked and the findings collected.
- Explain how user feedback influences the product.
- Assess whether the idea is ready for the next validation stage.

Evidence and findings should be documented as part of the project progress.

[Milestone 2 requirements](https://ocw.cs.pub.ro/courses/se/project/milestones/m2)

### M3 — Mockups & Landing Page

**Objective:** Present the product's initial user experience and visual direction.

This milestone focuses on translating the idea into an initial interface:

- Create and update wireframes for the main parts of the product.
- Present the planned user experience and navigation.
- Build a landing page that introduces the product to potential users.
- Use the landing page to communicate the product's purpose and attract interest.

The wireframes and landing page represent the current design direction and may change as the project is validated and developed.

[Milestone 3 requirements](https://ocw.cs.pub.ro/courses/se/project/milestones/m3)
