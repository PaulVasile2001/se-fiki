01 / THE WHY · M1

Good ideas shouldn't
get stuck alone.

Starting something new can be difficult when you have an idea but lack a co-founder, technical skills, or access to the right people.

Startup Helper aims to make it easier for aspiring founders and people with useful skills to discover each other and explore working together.

01↗

THE PROBLEM

Finding the right people is hard.

People may have promising startup ideas but struggle to find collaborators with complementary technical knowledge, experience, or motivation.

02✳

OUR SOLUTION

A place to connect.

Startup Helper is envisioned as a platform where people can share startup ideas, present their skills, and find potential collaborators.

03◎

VALUE PROPOSITION

From idea to collaboration.

Help idea owners and skilled contributors discover relevant people and take the first step towards building something together.
