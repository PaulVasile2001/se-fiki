01 / THE WHY

Good ideas shouldn't
get stuck alone.

Starting something new can be difficult when you have an idea but lack a co-founder, technical skills, or access to the right people.

Startup Helper aims to make it easier for aspiring founders and people with useful skills to discover each other and explore working together.
